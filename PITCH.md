# Pitch

**"She sold the piano. Silence stayed."**

Six words, one complete story: a piano — a lifetime of music, practice, and memories — is sold, and what remains is not the money or the empty room, but the silence itself, which lingers like a character. The turn lands in the second sentence: the silence doesn't leave with the piano, it stays. Every word earns its place, and the reader fills in the why: who she is, why she sold it, and what the silence sounds like now.
